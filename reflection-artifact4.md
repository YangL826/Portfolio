## Reflection — Artifact 4

For my fourth artifact, I used my Workshop 4 dialogue with Dot, the AI coach, on three data-challenge scenarios: missing data, noisy labels, and a credit-scoring model facing concept drift, imbalance, and fairness constraints together. The original conversation ran long and technical, so adapting it for a portfolio audience meant compressing each scenario down to the decisions that actually mattered and pulling out a single quotable insight per scenario, rather than reproducing the full transcript — a reader judging my competency needs the reasoning, not the raw dialogue. Working through the scenarios reinforced a pattern I hadn't named until I reached the third one and looked back: in every case, the instinct to reach for a fix was the trap, and the discipline was to first verify that I could measure whether the fix had actually worked — checking the missingness mechanism before imputing, building a gold-standard test set before training, recalibrating before optimizing. Dot's feedback pushed that further than I would have gone alone, particularly the human-override-rate metric in the noisy-labels scenario and the "rate of change matters more than a static threshold" point on production monitoring — both changed how I described my approach mid-conversation rather than just confirming what I already thought.
