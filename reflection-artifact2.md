## Reflection — Artifact 2

For my second artifact, I revised a class exercise from our onsite intensive in which a cohort peer interviewed me and introduced me to the class in his own words. I chose it because it shows a different skill than Artifact 1: instead of building an AI product, it shows I can own and adapt my own professional narrative. To make it a portfolio piece, I rewrote the introduction in the first person for a written audience rather than a spoken one, using what my peer remembered as a guide to what actually communicated clearly, and sharpening the parts he had flattened or generalized. The exercise reminded me that a story doesn't explain itself any more than a technical system does — both need to be tested with a real audience and revised based on what actually lands, not just what sounds right.
